Administrative Audit & Fair Allocation Principle

Statistical anomalies flagged by the Isolation Forest are mathematical deviations, NOT unilateral proof of fraud. All flagged transactions remain in review state until an authorized officer logs an official decision with written audit rationale. Original transaction records and ML anomaly scores are permanently preserved.

Understanding Isolation Forest Anomaly Scores • Continuous Distance, NOT Fraud Probability
Unsupervised Isolation Forest (100 Trees)
Score < 0 (Negative) Statistical Outlier

Points isolated with fewer tree splits (shorter path lengths) in the ensemble. Indicates abnormal quantity, off-hours timing, or irregular draw frequency.

Score ≥ 0 (Positive) Normal Inlier

Points requiring deep tree partitioning (longer path lengths). Conforms to standard registered cardholder quotas, operating hours, and standard distribution cadence.

Ethical AI Compliance Human In The Loop

Never treat score as a fraud percentage: A score of -0.38 is a geometric distance offset, NOT a 38% probability. Human verification with written comments is mandatory.
